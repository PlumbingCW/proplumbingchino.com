A sewer inspection in Chino, CA sends a waterproof camera on a push cable through your sewer line, from a cleanout or a pulled toilet to the street connection. The picture shows roots, cracks, offset joints, grease, scale and low spots where water pools. It also shows whether the pipe is clay, cast iron, plastic or something else, and where it runs.

The inspection is a planned service. It is the right move before you buy a home, after a repeated clog, before you spend money on landscaping, or when a plumber tells you the line needs repair and you want to see for yourself.

What the camera finds

Roots appear as hairy masses entering at joints. Cracks show as dark lines or missing pipe wall. A bellied section holds water, and the camera view floods and clears as the head passes. Grease looks like a pale lining, and scale in iron pipe looks like rough knuckles. A locator on the camera head lets the plumber mark the surface above a problem and measure the depth.

When to book an inspection

Book one when drains clog more than twice a year, when you buy a house built before the 1980s, when the yard smells or sinks, or after a tree near the line has been removed or has grown large. It is also worth doing before a large renovation that changes the bathroom or kitchen drains. Our sewer line repair page covers what happens if the camera finds a defect.

What you get after the visit

You should come away with a clear explanation of what the camera saw, the recording or screenshots if they are offered, a marked location, and options with prices if a fix is needed. If nothing is wrong, that is a result too, and it tells you a repeat clog has another cause, such as grease or a fixture trap.

Inspection for buyers and sellers

A general home inspector usually does not scope the sewer. A camera inspection before closing reveals problems that might cost thousands to fix, and it gives you leverage to negotiate. Ask for a written note on the pipe condition if you plan to use it in a deal.

Pairing with cleaning

Many lines need to be cleaned before the camera can see well. If the plumber runs hydro jetting first, the view is clearer. Expect the first pass to be a cleaning pass and the second to be the inspection.
