What Drives the Installed Cost of a Gas Water Heater in Chino, CA

Two quotes for the same tank can differ by a lot. The tank is only part of the price, and the rest depends on what your garage needs.

When people compare quotes for a gas water heater, they often compare the tank. The tank matters, but the installed price includes much more. It includes the labor to disconnect and haul away the old unit, the permit, the fittings and flexible lines, the venting, the safety valve, the seismic strapping and any code upgrades the garage needs.

This page does not quote dollar figures, because they change by market and by home. It explains what moves the price so that you can read a quote and ask the right questions.

The unit itself

Tank size, brand, efficiency rating and warranty length are the first drivers. A forty gallon tank costs less than a seventy five. A higher efficiency model with a longer warranty costs more up front and less to run. The uniform energy factor on the label compares efficiency across models.

Ask for the model number on the quote so that you can check it. Two quotes for the same size can be for very different units.

Venting and combustion air

A gas heater must vent its exhaust safely. Many garage installations use a Type B metal vent through the roof or wall. If the old vent is corroded, undersized or poorly sloped, it needs replacing. Some locations also require a certain amount of combustion air.

A heater in a closet or a living space has stricter rules than one in an open garage. A different location can add cost.

Code upgrades

Water heaters in California must be braced against earthquakes with straps, and the work needs to meet current code. Other common items are an expansion tank on closed systems, a new pressure and temperature relief valve with a drain line, a gas shutoff, a sediment trap on the gas line and a drain pan where leaks could damage floors.

If the existing install does not meet code, expect the quote to include corrections. The permit inspection checks them.

Access, removal and permit

A heater in a tight attic or on a raised platform takes more labor than one on a garage floor. Old tank removal, disposal and the permit fee are line items. A heater that has leaked into the surrounding area may need repairs to the pan or flooring.

How to compare quotes

Ask each plumber for an itemized quote that lists the unit and model, venting, gas work, relief valve, strapping, permit, haul away and warranty. Ask whether the plumber is licensed, and check at cslb.ca.gov. Our water heater installation page and water heater replacement page describe what a complete job includes.

Warranty and long-term cost

The warranty on the tank covers the tank, and labor is often separate. Ask what the installer covers and for how long. A higher efficiency tank that costs more up front may repay the difference in lower gas use over the years, but only if it lasts. Maintenance such as flushing the tank once a year helps it last longer.

Ask also about the anode rod. It is the part that corrodes in place of the tank, and replacing it as it wears can add years to the heater's life. Many installers will show you how to check it.

Questions that sort good quotes from vague ones

Ask whether the quote includes the permit, venting changes, strapping, the relief valve drain and removal of the old unit. Ask for the model number, the warranty terms and an expected completion time. A quote that names each item gives you something to compare. A single line with a total price does not.

Safety steps before an old gas heater comes out

Turn the gas control to off and close the gas shutoff valve before anyone works on the heater. Shut the cold water supply too. Do not store paint, gasoline or solvents near the new heater, because the burner can ignite their vapors.

Water heater permits and earthquakes

Chino Hills sits in earthquake country. The Chino fault runs along the city's eastern edge and the Whittier fault along the west, and a magnitude 5.4 earthquake struck in 2008. That is why strapping is a standard part of any install. The City of Chino Building Division publishes a water heater strapping handout, and its office at 13220 Central Avenue, Chino, CA 91710, can be reached at (909) 334-3251.

Ask the division how your replacement will be handled, because permit rules vary by address. Our Chino Hills page lists coverage for the west side.

Installations follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).
