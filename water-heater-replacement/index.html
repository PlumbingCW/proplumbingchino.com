Water heater replacement in Chino, CA is the right answer when the tank has corroded through, when repairs keep piling up, or when the unit is old enough that the next failure is only a matter of time. A replacement job includes shutting off water and gas or power, draining and removing the old unit, setting the new one, reconnecting supply, venting and safety devices, and testing before the plumber leaves.

People call us in two situations. Some have a flooded closet at midnight and need hot water back fast. Others know the heater is aging and want a planned swap on their schedule.

When a failed water heater is an emergency

A tank that is pouring water, a heater with a gas smell, or a household with infants or elderly residents and no hot water at all is an emergency. Until the plumber arrives, protect the house with these steps.

  1. Smell gas? Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  2. Turn off the power at the breaker, or set a gas unit's control to off.
  3. Shut the cold water valve above the heater and open one hot tap to relieve pressure.
  4. Lift anything valuable off the floor near the tank and keep outlets dry.
  5. Take a photo of the damage and the tank's data label, which lists its age and capacity.

Repair or replace? A practical rule

A water heater past 10 years with a leak from the tank body is a replacement, full stop. A younger unit with a failed element or thermostat is a repair. In between, the decision comes down to the total cost of repairs against a new unit and how many things are failing. Our water heater repair page lists what can be fixed.

The anode rod is a hint. It is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the tank does not. When it is gone, the steel starts to go. Checking it every few years extends life, but once the tank wall is compromised, nothing restores it.

Sizing the new tank

Tank capacity should match how your household uses hot water. Two people usually do fine on a 40 gallon tank, while a family of four with a soaking tub often wants 50 gallons or more. Peak demand matters more than the total number of residents. Tell the plumber when showers, laundry and the dishwasher overlap.

Energy labels list a first hour rating, which tells you how much hot water the unit delivers in the first hour of heavy use. That figure is more useful than the capacity printed on the front.

What a replacement includes

Beyond the tank itself, a good install replaces flexible supply connectors, fits a new temperature and pressure relief valve discharge line, checks the expansion tank where one is needed, and confirms venting on gas units. The thermostat should be set at 120 degrees Fahrenheit unless there is a reason otherwise, which cuts scald risk and standby loss.

Water heaters in California are strapped against earthquake movement, and the City of Chino Building Division publishes a water heater strapping handout. We cover permit rules for your address in the next section.

Options: tank, tankless or heat pump

Tank gas and electric units remain the lowest upfront cost. Tankless units save space and heat water on demand. Heat pump water heaters use far less electricity but need room and airflow. Our tankless water heater page compares running costs and maintenance.

Safety steps when a water heater fails

If the tank is leaking, shut off the cold water supply to it and turn the gas control to off or switch off its breaker. Do not touch a tank with water around electrical parts. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

If you are planning ahead rather than reacting, a scheduled swap lets you pick the unit instead of taking whatever is available. See water heater installation for new-unit and relocation work, and plumbing installation for fixtures and valves that are worth updating at the same visit.

A replacement tank must meet the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, UPC) for bracing, relief valve discharge and venting, and gas storage units are built to ANSI Z21.10.1.
