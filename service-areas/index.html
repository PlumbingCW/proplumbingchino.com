Chino ZIP codes and what we cover

Chino uses ZIP codes 91708 and 91710. The 91710 area includes the Central Avenue corridor and the commercial centers along it. This site covers residential and commercial plumbing across both ZIP codes, from older homes near downtown to newer tracts.

Chino was first subdivided in 1887 and incorporated in 1910, so the housing stock spans more than a century. Older homes tend to have aging drain lines and corroded supply pipe, while homes from later decades have their own issues, such as original water heaters and fixtures. Our drain cleaning and water heater repair pages describe how each is handled.

Chino Hills, Ontario, Pomona and Montclair in more detail

Chino Hills was incorporated on December 1, 1991, and counted 78,411 residents in the 2020 census. Most of its housing went up in a quick burst of building in the 1980s and 1990s, so water heaters, shutoff valves and original supply lines tend to age in the same years. Our page on emergency plumber Chino Hills goes through it neighborhood by neighborhood.

Ontario sits to the northeast and is home to Ontario International Airport and the Ontario Mills shopping center, so commercial calls there are common. Pomona, incorporated in 1888, is one of the older cities in the area, with Cal Poly Pomona and the Fairplex fairgrounds, and many older homes where drain and supply lines have seen decades of use.

Montclair lies just north of Chino and Pomona, around Montclair Place and the transit center. Across all of these, the questions callers ask are alike: where is my main shutoff, what can I do first, and what will it cost.

Before you call from any city

Have your street address and nearest cross street ready, along with a call-back number. Tell us what is wet, blocked or broken, and whether water is still running. If it is, close the main water valve first. It is usually near the street side of the house, at the water meter, or on the outside wall near the water heater.

Take a quick photo of the damage if it is safe to do so. Note the time you first noticed the problem. It helps the plumber arrive with the right parts, and it helps if you later make an insurance claim. Move valuables away from the water and keep children and pets out of the area. If you are a landlord or manager, note who can let the plumber in and whether tenants are home.
