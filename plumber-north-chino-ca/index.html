Where North Chino is

The City of Chino General Plan treats the part of the city north of Schaefer Avenue as mostly residential, with commercial corridors along Central Avenue, Riverside Drive and Philadelphia Street. State Route 60 crosses the northern part of the city, and most of the city's historic buildings are in this area.

The General Plan describes the northern neighborhoods as older, predominantly residential areas with ranch-style and mid-century homes on larger lots. Homes nearer downtown tend to be smaller single-family houses. North Chino uses ZIP code 91710.

Why larger lots change the plumbing

On a larger lot the sewer lateral from the house to the street is generally longer, and more of it passes under yard, driveway and landscaping. When a main line backs up, the cleanout is usually the starting point. Our sewer line cleaning page explains what a cleaning visit involves.

If clogs return after cleaning, ask for a camera check. A line that keeps clogging may have a low spot, a break or intruding roots, and the camera shows which. For heavy buildup, hydro jetting uses high pressure water to scour the pipe wall.

Yard leaks, hose bibs and outbuildings

Wet ground with no rain, a lawn patch that stays green in a dry week or a meter that keeps moving can all point to a yard leak. Leak detection finds the break without digging up the whole yard. Exterior faucets can also fail, and faucet repair covers hose bibs and worn handles.

A garage, workshop or back house with its own water line adds more pipe to check. If a line to a detached space fails, a plumber can trace it from the main and quote a repair.

Cleanouts, access and what to have ready

Know where your sewer cleanout is, and keep it clear. A visible cleanout lets a plumber reach the line without pulling a toilet. If you cannot find one, say so when you call, and the plumber will look for another access point.

For bigger projects, ask the City of Chino Building Division about permits at (909) 334-3251. The office is at 13220 Central Avenue.

Before you call from North Chino

1. Find the cleanout

Look near the house or at the property line for a capped pipe at ground level.

2. Check the meter

Turn off fixtures and see if the meter still moves, which suggests a leak.

3. Stop the water

Close the main valve if water is pooling in the yard or inside.

4. Mark the wet spot

Photograph it and note the date so the plumber knows where to start.
